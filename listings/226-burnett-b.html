The Neighborhood

Old Louisville is one of the city's most beloved historic districts – known for its tree-lined streets, Victorian architecture, and vibrant community. You'll be within walking distance of:

Central Park – concerts, walking paths, and green space
St. James Court Art Show – one of the nation's largest outdoor art fairs
University of Louisville – just minutes away
Local cafes & restaurants – walkable options on every corner
Arts & culture – galleries, theaters, and historic mansions
Easy access – to downtown and major roadways
